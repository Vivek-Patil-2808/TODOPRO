    About TodoPro

    TodoPro is a modern, productivity-focused task management application built for users who want a simple yet powerful way to organize their daily work. It helps you manage tasks efficiently with priorities, due dates, and a distraction-free interface. Our goal is to make task management easy, organized, and visually pleasing. Whether it’s personal goals, academic work, or professional tasks, TodoPro ensures everything stays clear and manageable in one place. Built using modern web technologies, TodoPro focuses on performance, responsiveness, and smooth user interactions — enhanced with clean animations, intuitive controls, and dark & light mode support.
